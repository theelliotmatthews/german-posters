# Gender Worlds — Food test (ESSEN)

Three illustrated learning posters. Each gender is a complete world. The colour of the world is the memory trick: pink = DIE, blue = DER, green = DAS. Every noun sits in the scene as a real object with a hanging label.

Not the school-chart series. Cartoony-illustrative, toy-catalog lighting, sharp type.

---

## SERIES LOCK

Vertical 4:5 educational picture poster, 1536×1920, designed graphic artefact.

One idea: the WORLD COLOUR is the gender. If it lives in this world, it takes that article.

Type system: two families only.
- Display: heavy condensed grotesque, all caps, for the title only.
- Labels: clean geometric sans, mixed case, high contrast on small cream tickets.

Every food object has one cream ticket label with EXACT TEXT only: German article + noun on line 1, English gloss on line 2. No extra words. No duplicate labels. No unlabelled foods. Correct German umlauts. Article printed in the world colour.

No dead-centre symmetry. No teal-and-orange grade. No lens flare. No floating bokeh dust. No logos, no brand mascots, no copyrighted characters. Paper-print grain in the sky only, not over type.
