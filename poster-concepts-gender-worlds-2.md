# Gender Worlds II — Appliances, Vehicles, Household, Sports

Same rule as food: pink = DIE, blue = DER, green = DAS. One crowded labelled scene per gender. Cartoony-illustrative, toy-catalog light, cream tickets, German then English. No logos, no mascots, no extra unlabelled objects.

```
SERIES LOCK. Vertical 4:5 educational picture poster, 1536x1920, designed graphic artefact. World colour is the gender: pink DIE, blue DER, green DAS. Cream ticket labels, German article+noun then English. No extra words, no duplicate labels, no unlabelled objects, no logos, no mascots. Paper grain in sky only. Sharp type.
```
