# BAUSTEINE VISUAL — pictogram companion charts

Visual companion to the text-only Bausteine wall. Same content jobs, but every item gets a flat pictogram or mini-scene so abstract glue words stick.

Generate with:
`python3 scripts/generate.py --concepts poster-concepts-bausteine-visual.md --outdir output-bausteine-visual`

```
SERIES LOCK. Vertical 4:5 designed educational poster artefact, 1536×1920, not a photo of a poster on a wall.

Style anchor: Swiss school chart meets flat pictogram vocabulary poster (like a 1968 wall chart upgraded with simple icons). Cream newsprint ground #EFE8D8, carbon type #141414, brick-red section bars #8B1E1E under 10% area, English gloss warm grey #5C574E.

Type: heavy condensed grotesque all caps for title only; clean geometric sans for labels. German bold black, English smaller grey under each German line.

Icons: flat 2D pictograms, solid fill, no gradients, no 3D, no photos, no cartoon faces, no logos. Each vocabulary item has ONE simple icon left of the text. Icons must match meaning (thermometer for hot/cold, arrow for direction, speech bubble for phrases).

Layout: asymmetric grid with brick-red section bars. Hairline rules between rows. Tiny plate numbers top right. Generous margins. Paper grain in margins only.

Readable at arm's length. Sharp kerning. Correct German umlauts. Exact quoted text only — no invented words, no duplicate lines, no extra labels.
```
