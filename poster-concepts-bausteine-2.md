# BAUSTEINE 03–04 — verbs and opposites

The wall already has nouns, grammar, connectors, and survival phrases. Two gaps left that you actually use every hour:

1. A verb list. Without 100 workhorse verbs you can name things and still not say anything.
2. Opposites. This is how you describe size, time, price, mood, weather, and quality.
